## Executive profile

My research is on the intersection of artificial intelligence for galaxy studies in astrophysics, with a particular focus on low surface brightness and dwarf galaxies. I care about general AI in Astrophysics papers that use computer vision on galaxies, or use agentic AI to do a novel analysis, particularly interested in computer vision, agentic harnessses, uncertainty quantification, physics incorporated or informed models/ differential simulation, explainability/interpretability research. I also care about cosmology simulation results that have new predictions for dwarf galaxy observations (photometry or spectroscopy) conditioned on new physics or resolutions. I care about datasets or tools that may have been published that would allow me to model individual stars (isochrone or stellar templates) in a stellar population synthesis sort of direction (including nebular emission, binary systems, extremes of hot young stars of white dwarfs, etc.).
